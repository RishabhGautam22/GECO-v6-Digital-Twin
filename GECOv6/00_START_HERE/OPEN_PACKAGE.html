The findings in ordinary language

Simpler forecast choices performed better on the supplied records. A more complex monitor was not consistently better at balancing extra delivery against withheld delivery. The primary second-model branch had important physiological-domain failures. A separate fasting study removed those declared failures but still found benefits, costs and false stops. The paper makes a bounded simulation and methods claim.
