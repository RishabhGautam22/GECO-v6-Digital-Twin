## Evidence vocabulary

SIMULATION RESULT means a completed recorded numerical experiment. CONCEPTUAL RIG means a proposed illustration, with component selection and dimensional fit pending. IIT PATNA MEASUREMENT PENDING means no laboratory value exists yet; cells must not contain invented zeros or plausible estimates. IMPLEMENTED means source is present; VALIDATED always names the actual test and its scope. Publication suitability is an editorial assessment; no IEEE, MIT or journal endorsement is implied.
