# Prespecified offline comparison

The primary experiment estimates the effect of delivery containment/recovery while holding the underlying requested-command tape fixed. A clean feedback reference, generated with the production ResearchController and corrected PhysiologyTwin, supplies identical requested commands to every paired arm. Meals, pump noise, sensor noise and balance noise are also paired. The intervention can inhibit or cap authorization, while physical faults alter actual emission. This is not a claim about autonomous feedback-controller efficacy. A smaller feedback sensitivity run allows each arm's own sensor/twin to change requests and uses that arm's clean feedback run as reference.

The immutable preregistration and generated parameter split precede outcomes. There are two plant families, each with8 calibration and24 locked synthetic parameter units and two repeated exogenous seeds. Body weights and six synthetic factors are generated independently; these are engineering variations, not a fitted patient population. Seeds within a unit are not independent participant replications. The main matrix is48 test units×2seeds×8conditions×6arms=4608runs, each48h at5min. The feedback sensitivity is12units×2seeds×4conditions×2arms×3stop assumptions=576runs.

The structurally distinct Hovorka implementation, equations/units, primary-source table verification and numerical limitations are documented separately. The original-family plant uses the production GlucoseModel. The study twin imports the newly corrected production PhysiologyTwin and its selected causal forecast policy, applies the fresh calibration configuration, and receives current reported CGM with prior authorized delivery and prior carbohydrate inputs. It never receives evaluator glucose or emitted mass. Forecasts do not inspect future meals or insulin. A pooled calibration is an explicitly described transfer model; no individually calibrated synthetic twin is claimed.

The simple detector is a single-step absolute reported-mass residual. The full study adapter combines a delivery CUSUM with corroboration from one physiology group. Standardized innovation and due forecast residual are transforms of the same CGM stream and select one strongest signed input to that group; they are not counted as separate witnesses. Its raw score is max(massCUSUM,sqrt(massCUSUM×physiologyCUSUM)). Both CUSUMs use drift0.5 and input clipping±6. This is an explicit new study adapter built on the corrected filter, not a claim to have exercised the complete legacy protocol/kernel implementation.

Separate full/simple thresholds strictly exceed their maximum statistic over32 clean calibration runs. The zero empirical calibration-run statistical containment count is a tuning outcome, not a population guarantee. Locked clean false containment is reported, including common local/quality trips. Both arms have the same balance/report availability, command cap, local rolling budget, sensor missing rule and physical stop assumptions. Report masking leaves the local monitor enabled and available in every arm. All fault conditions are ordinary offline numerical perturbations of existing physical/sensor abstractions; no packet, radio, firmware, credential, intrusion or hardware work occurs.

Recovery is explicitly simulated supervision. A fixed120-minute operator schedule is identical across arms and independent of the fault label/end. After30minutes latched, the monitor needs six quiet local-mass observations and an available current CGM. Manual emulation then clears the latch. Supervised emulation first caps three verification requests at0.08researchU (never above clean demand); it requires aggregate measured mass within0.012g of expected calibrated mass and available CGM, then six similarly capped probation intervals. No catch-up delivery is accumulated. This limited measurement resolution can wrongly qualify a fault or fail to clear a repaired device; all such outcomes are retained. The offline oracle separately knows the physical fault window and inhibits its delivery. It is privileged and is not used by either practical recovery rule.

The main stop assumption inhibits future powered delivery after a trip observed at a sample's end. Emission already produced in that interval cannot be removed. The feedback sensitivity adds either one more full five-minute interval of power or an unpowered residual tail of0.02researchU for three intervals. These are prespecified engineering assumptions, not measured subsecond trip times or validated fluid behavior.

Timing clarification before outcomes: first-trip latency uses the end of the trip interval. Commanded latch exposure uses the state before each emission interval; the count of latched end states is separate. Commanded latch is not synonymous with flow cessation when delayed power or residual flow remains. Recovery onset is the start of the first six-interval qualifying window; its observed confirmation endpoint is30minutes later. The restricted recovery-latency metric uses onset, with censoring retained.

The cooldown also begins at the trip interval's end: a95-minute trip cannot reset at the120-minute operator slot because only25minutes elapsed. Hovorka x3>1 is recorded as negative endogenous production and fails the numerical/domain screen; the original equation remains unchanged. `plant_valid` is a screening field, not proof of physiological validity. Original-family interstitial and action clipping have separate counts beyond its plasma-domain screen.

Outcomes compare actual emitted delivery with the paired clean physical reference, retaining stepwise excess, deficits and signed net differences. Interruption requires positive underlying demand with no emission; total latch duration is also recorded. Recovery is the first six-interval post-fault window without latch and with at least95% authorization, with some positive demand. Unrecovered cases retain censoring and a restricted remaining-horizon value for descriptive paired inference. Glucose bands and paired error are calculated from evaluator plant state, never reported CGM. Negative/out-of-domain Hovorka states and original-model clipping boundaries are flagged; glucose summaries on such trajectories are mathematical outputs with physiological validity unresolved.

Every run writes a complete trace and attempt record. Exceptions are not omitted or replaced silently. Inference is the mean paired difference, averaging both repeated seeds within parameter unit, followed by4096 percentile bootstrap resamples of those units. Families, conditions, policies and stop assumptions remain separate. Intervals are descriptive synthetic-design uncertainty, with no multiplicity-adjusted superiority or patient generalization. Video examples use the first locked unit/seed fixed by index, not a favorable outcome selected after the run.

Corrected runtime/calibration must be frozen before prepare. Exact executed commands, source hashes, configuration hashes and complete attempts take precedence over this abbreviated usage text.
