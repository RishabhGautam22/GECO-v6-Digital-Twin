# Primary comparative release interpretation

The complete primary release contains4608 fixed-clean-command intervention runs and576 separately defined feedback/stop-sensitivity runs, with no run failures. All48 synthetic test parameter units and both repeated seeds remain present. The software trace audit checks5184complete traces and2,985,984samples. An independent science-lane audit recomputed37 numeric/integrity fields per trace without the engine/metric helpers and found zero discrepancies; see `08_REVIEW_AND_QA/filter/study_cross_review/TRACE_AUDIT.json`. Failed verification counts are retained from execution metadata but cannot always be independently reconstructed from the trace's single-priority trip reason.

The main comparison does not show a general advantage for the full adapter. Under supervised-reset emulation, stuck-on excess emission is greater for full than simple by0.13745researchU (95%parameter-unit interval0.10044to0.17445) in the original-family plant and0.08458U (0.04229to0.12688) in Hovorka. Full may reduce some delivery deficits while permitting more excess; that tradeoff is not a unidirectional benefit. In Hovorka occlusion, full-supervised increases deficit by1.13031U (0.13982to2.37804) relative to simple-supervised. All intervals are descriptive, resample synthetic parameter units after averaging repeated seeds, and are not multiplicity-adjusted clinical claims.

Zero empirical calibration statistical containment does not guarantee a matched future rate. The locked original-family clean runs have0/48trips in each arm. Hovorka clean runs have2/48trips for each full policy and0/48for each simple policy, sham and oracle. The sham retains common local cutoff/quality rules, so differences are not obtained by giving full stronger physical stop authority. Full versus simple also changes mass aggregation as well as physiology corroboration; this primary comparison alone cannot isolate the physiology channel's incremental contribution.

The physiological-domain limitation is substantial:2108/4608main runs fail the numerical/domain screen. Hovorka clean runs fail in44/48cases per arm, often because the original equation's endogenous production becomes negative before glucose itself becomes negative. The original-family clean runs have no plasma-domain failures, but100remote-action boundary samples per arm remain. `plant_valid` denotes a numerical/domain screen, not physiological validation. Glucose-band differences, particularly for Hovorka, must be treated as mathematical stress-model outputs. They cannot establish patient benefit, clinical safety or robust physiological generalization.

The command-tape estimand holds the underlying clean controller requests fixed across paired arms. The feedback sensitivity allows requests to diverge under each arm's own observations and is a separate policy estimand. Commanded latch exposure uses pre-emission state; first-trip latency uses interval end; delayed power or residual flow can persist after a commanded latch. Recovery is authorization-based, with onset and confirmation endpoints separated; it does not certify device repair or physical function.

All adverse/null results and invalid trajectories are retained in `RUN_RESULTS`, `ARM_SUMMARIES` and `PAIRED_PARAMETER_UNIT_CI`, together with clean references, traces and complete attempts. Any subsequent fasting or component-ablation study is supplemental and must not replace these primary results.
