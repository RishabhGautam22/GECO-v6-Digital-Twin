# V6 causal filter and forecast calibration: prospective analysis rules

Committed before new V6 fit or forecast outcomes, 2026-09-07. This is a new analysis of previously exposed supplied data, not a new external holdout or clinical validation. The V5 release remains immutable. The companion `PREREGISTRATION_RECEIPT.json` commits this specification and the original input bytes. Amendments must be dated and retain the original receipt.

## Population, chronology, and information boundary

Enumerate every participant from the supplied HUPA-UCM and D1NAMO loaders without selecting on performance. Use exact index cut points floor(0.50 N) and floor(0.75 N), with disjoint chronological fit, tune, and locked test windows. A participant is eligible when fit contains at least 288 five-minute slots, tune/test each at least 144, and each window has at least 50% observed CGM and at least 50 eligible forecast origin/target pairs after a 36-slot warmup. All exclusions and fit failures remain in the manifest. Dataset resampling, HUPA preprocessing, D1NAMO uniformly allocated slow insulin and calorie-derived carbohydrate proxies remain limitations. No new physiological ground truth is inferred from these records.

Inputs logged in slot k are treated as events in the following interval: observation k is assimilated using the previous slot's insulin/carbohydrate input, with zero previous input at the start of each independent window. This is the same convention used by the V6 synthetic study. Forecasts at k use CGM and logged inputs through k only. Mechanistic forecasts hold a basal amount fixed from the fit window (median supplied basal, including zeros), and assume zero future meals. They never use future logged events. No future CGM is used to initialize a window. After at least 12 missing slots (60 minutes), reappearance initializes from that current observation and clears innovation, trend, and outstanding-forecast memory. Missing observations break the AR(1) pair; pre-gap forecasts may score only if they mature before a long-gap reset. Initialization/reset observations are not innovations.

## Numerical repair and fitting

Use nominal observation variance in the gain and Joseph covariance update. Innovation calibration changes anomaly normalization only, never the state or covariance. Observation variance depends on the predicted observation range. Validate finite symmetric positive-semidefinite covariance; small numerical negative eigenvalues are projected to zero, while material invalid covariance causes a recorded reset rather than propagation. Reset flushes all residual memory. Fixed Q and nominal R retain their original values; their clinical interpretation is not claimed.

Fit equilibrium glucose, p1, kc and p3 on the fit window, with log bounds [80,320], [0.0001,0.03], [0.05,3], [1e-7,0.005], and egp = equilibrium * p1. Other parameters remain declared defaults, except fit-window reference glucose and basal steady insulin. Do not impose the 1800-rule clinical prior. Two deterministic starts use kc 0.4 and 1.2, p1 0.006 and p3 0.00006. Bounded SciPy least_squares minimizes the vector of causal 30-minute mechanistic forecast errors at every sixth eligible origin, max_nfev=80 per start (or the explicit configured max_iter value); ftol=xtol=gtol=0.001. Use the lowest finite objective among optimizer-converged starts; if none converge, record failure and do not freeze that participant. Report starts, evaluations, termination, bound proximity and local Jacobian singular values. Local conditioning is a diagnostic, not proof of parameter identification. Performance reports use every eligible five-minute origin, not only the objective subsample. Optional compilation may accelerate exactly the same arithmetic and must be parity checked.

## Baselines, selection, and uncertainty calibration

Compare three deployable 30-minute CGM forecasts on identical finite origin/target masks: mechanistic; persistence at the last observed CGM; and a trend using ordinary least squares over up to four immediately preceding consecutive observed CGM values, with slope clipped to +/-3 mg/dL/min and multiplied by 0.5 over the horizon. Fewer than two consecutive values gives persistence. Forecast outputs are bounded to [20,600] mg/dL, matching the model domain. Trend settings and policy set are fixed here. Select the lowest tune-window RMSE, ties within 1e-9 resolved persistence, trend, mechanistic. The selected policy and its hold amount are frozen before any locked-test scores are produced. Report all three and selected-policy scores; do not tune again after test.

Estimate AR(1) rho only from adjacent valid fit-window nominal innovations after warmup, clipped to [0,0.98] (zero when insufficient or constant). Set innovation inflation to (95th percentile absolute AR-whitened nominal z / 1.96)^2, floored at 1e-6; use the same nominal filter, rho and inflation on tune/test and runtime. Set selected-forecast sigma6 to fit-window 95th percentile absolute selected-policy error /1.96, floored at 1 mg/dL. These are empirical calibration factors, not Gaussian/independence guarantees. Report actual tune/test coverage and residual autocorrelation.

For each physiology channel, search a fixed ascending threshold grid 4 to 128 inclusive in increments 0.5, drift 0.5, for at most 0.5 benign alarm episodes per calendar day. Use runtime detector episode/reset semantics. Record feasibility, observed slots, calendar and observed-equivalent duration, channel alarm count/rates, and the full candidate search. An empty or less-than-50%-covered channel is ineligible. No feasible candidate is a calibration failure, never a ceiling labeled success. Report the union of channel alarm timestamps separately; a tuning budget does not establish prospective sensitivity or availability. Thresholds are not tuned on attacks or locked test.

## Locked evaluation and reproducibility

The fit/tune runner writes all participant settings and failures and a SHA-256 freeze receipt before a separate test-evaluation invocation. Record source/data/array hashes, segment boundaries/timestamps, exact command, Python/library versions, elapsed time, and optimizer diagnostics. Model identity is unknown unless explicitly exposed. Locked test remains previously exposed internal data. Primary metrics: participant RMSE, MAE, forecast pair count/coverage, selected policy, standardized-innovation and selected-forecast empirical 95% coverage, alarm episodes/calendar day and observed-equivalent day. Report equal-participant aggregate medians and paired mean RMSE differences with participant bootstrap 95% intervals (10,000 resamples, seed 20260907), descriptive only. Report missing/failure counts and all negative/null results. No clinical superiority or security-detection improvement follows from forecast performance alone.

## Required countertests

Run original regression suite and independent integration review.
