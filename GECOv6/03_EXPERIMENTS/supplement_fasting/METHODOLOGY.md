# Post-primary fasting and component supplement

This is a follow-on exploratory study. Its design was selected after the original primary results revealed frequent Hovorka domain failures; the original 4,608 fixed-tape and 576 feedback/stop sensitivity outcomes remain unchanged. The supplemental operating-regime hypothesis came from the original **calibration traces only**: no Hovorka negative endogenous production occurred before the first meal, whereas all 16 Hovorka calibration traces exhibited negative production later. This observation motivated an explicitly narrower fasting experiment; it does not validate the earlier meal regime or rescue a superiority claim.

The new specification and synthetic vector split were committed locally at the timestamp in PREREGISTRATION_COMMIT.json, before any new supplemental calibration or test outcome. This is a local computational commitment, not registration with an external registry. There are four new calibration and 16 new locked test vectors per plant, independently generated using parameter seed 202609072 and the same declared parameter distributions. Noise seeds 3907 and 4801 are repeated within each vector. Neither these vectors nor their synthetic held-out status represent independent patients.

Every run has 576 five-minute intervals with no meals. The constant requested amount is chosen by inverting only the known noise-free nominal actuator mapping: 200 steps per research U, gain 1.015, and 0.0015-g dribble on positive quantized delivery. The candidate set contains zero and the adjacent positive microsteps, capped at 0.30 research U. The candidate with smallest absolute error relative to the plant's analytically calculated equilibrium basal is selected; ties select the smaller request. No simulated glucose trajectory is used to choose or tune this command. The noise clamp can add a small positive bias that is retained, not fitted away. Requested/authorized/emitted totals, target basal, nominal quantization error, 48-hour bias, and zero-command counts are explicit outputs.

The 2,048-run matrix is two plants × 16 locked vectors × two repeated seeds × eight supplied offline conditions × four supervised arms. All arms receive identical fixed requests and exogenous random arrays, and retain the same command cap, local measurement, local budgets, missing-sensor rule, next-sample emission stop, operator schedule, verification and probation. The sham disables statistical trips but retains those shared rules. The mass-CUSUM-only arm uses exactly the full arm's mass accumulator, drift, clipping, mass scale and reset logic, with no physiology input. The full arm calls the corrected production PhysiologyTwin and uses the already transferred fresh-calibration modal forecast policy. Its physiology channel uses prior-slot monitor-visible inputs; it never receives evaluator emission or evaluator glucose. The selected policy and production hashes are preserved in FROZEN_SUPPLEMENT_CONFIG.json.

Full, simple and mass-CUSUM thresholds are each strictly above their score maximum on the same 16 clean calibration runs. This provides equal calibration exposure and zero empirical calibration statistical containment. It does not establish a population false-alarm bound, and the locked clean-run false containments are reported. The clean reference disables interventions while preserving the same fixed request and noise arrays; arm-minus-clean outcomes therefore estimate changes caused by delivery authority on that fixed command sequence.

The six registered paired contrasts are full minus mass-CUSUM, mass-CUSUM minus simple, full minus simple, and each of full/mass-CUSUM/simple minus sham. The first contrast isolates the specified physiology-corroboration policy component together with its separately calibrated threshold, rather than validating physiology as an independent biomarker. Two seeds are averaged within vector before 4,096 bootstrap resamples of the 16 vectors, separately by plant and condition. Confidence intervals are descriptive, exploratory, and not multiplicity-adjusted. All null and adverse results remain in the tables.

Timing and physical semantics are inherited unchanged from the primary corrected engine. A trip is an interval-end decision; the earliest stop is the next interval. Recovery requires at least 30 minutes from the trip-end boundary plus fixed 120-minute operator authorization, six quiet local measurements and current CGM availability. Verification/probation remain bounded and no missed delivery is repaid. Recovery latency reports the onset of the first six-interval qualifying post-fault window, with confirmation 30 minutes later. Neither recovery nor the controller inspects fault-end labels or evaluator truth. This is explicitly simulated supervised-reset authorization, not an autonomous patient policy.

All outcomes and domain flags are retained without exclusions. Negative EGP and numerical/domain validity are reported separately from original-family clamp boundaries. A trajectory that passes this limited numerical/domain screen is not thereby a physiologically validated patient trajectory. Fasting stability does not resolve meal-excited failures, generalize to clinical use, or establish measured rig dynamics. The primary delay/tail and feedback sensitivities remain separate; no extra supplemental feedback or delayed-stop matrix is implied.
