All 1,179,648 outcome slots passed the prespecified numerical/domain screen, including zero negative-EGP samples. This finding concerns the narrower fasting constant-request regime and does not resolve or replace the original meal-excited domain failures. The 16 supplemental clean calibration runs also had zero domain flags. Nominal compensation selected a positive command for every test vector: zero of 32 vectors and zero of 2,048 runs used a compensated zero request. Quantization residuals ranged from −0.00247666 to +0.00291449 research U per five-minute interval; actual requested, authorized and emitted costs are retained separately.

Passing the declared screen must not be summarized as absence of model constraints or normal glycemia. The original-family model recorded 18,648 remote-action boundary samples despite zero plasma/interstitial clamp samples. Across all four arms, Hovorka clean glucose ranged from 74.03 to 203.03 mg/dL, and all-condition glucose ranged from 67.49 to 427.43 mg/dL. Original-family clean glucose ranged from 111.50 to 161.98 mg/dL. These numerical model outputs expose remaining operating-regime and quantization sensitivity; no clinical validity follows from finite states or nonnegative EGP.

Matching clean calibration exposure did not eliminate locked clean false containment. In the original-family plant, full, mass-CUSUM and simple each tripped in 3/32 clean runs. In Hovorka, full tripped in 3/32, versus 2/32 for mass-CUSUM and simple. Sham tripped in 0/32 clean runs for each plant. Mean full-arm delivery deficits relative to the clean reference were 0.09373 research U in the original family and 0.06561 U in Hovorka. These observations demonstrate finite-sample transfer limits of the run-maximum tuning criterion; they are not a validated alarm-rate guarantee.

The added component comparison showed a conditional reduction in excessive emission and an adverse sensor-only cost. Under doubled emission plus a +25 mg/dL reported sensor bias, full minus mass-CUSUM reduced excess by 0.07073 research U (95% paired vector-bootstrap interval, −0.09821 to −0.04298) in the original family and by 0.04187 U (−0.07945 to −0.00952) in Hovorka. Under sensor bias alone, it increased delivery deficit by 1.33868 U (0.71855 to 2.10441) and 0.39818 U (0.20600 to 0.60903), respectively. Excess-only conditions also included adverse or null full-versus-mass contrasts, and no global dominance follows. All six registered contrasts, all eight conditions, glucose-band changes, interruption, recovery censoring and costs are retained in the complete tables.

Neither estimates patient efficacy: all four arms share the same fixed requests, sensing, local authority and simulated supervised-reset authorization.
